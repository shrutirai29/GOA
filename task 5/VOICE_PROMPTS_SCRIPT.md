## 💡 Quick Tips for the Recording

- **Keep Wispr Flow Visible:** Make sure the Wispr Flow floating widget or cursor transcription is visible on your screen while you speak so reviewers can verify real-time voice input.
- **Microphone Quality:** Use a headset or dedicated mic for crisp audio with minimal background noise.
- **Pacing:** Speak at a natural, conversational pace. Wispr Flow handles normal conversational speed effortlessly!
